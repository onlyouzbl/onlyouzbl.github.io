About the Lab

The Multimodal Understanding and Retrieval Lab (LIN-AI Lab) was founded at Ningbo University on November 5, 2026. Led by Bolin Zhang, the lab focuses on multimodal retrieval. We study how a query in one form, such as a sentence, can help us find relevant information in another, such as a video.

Research Areas

Video Moment Retrieval from Large Video Collections

Given a description such as “a person pushes a bicycle across the street,” we want to find both the right video in a collection and the exact interval it describes. We study how to distinguish similar actions and learn their boundaries when training examples have captions but no timestamps.

Video Anomaly Detection

We study how to identify moments in long recordings that warrant a closer look. Context matters: running on a track may mean something different from running through a crowded station.

Spiking Neural Networks for Video

Consecutive frames often contain much of the same information. We explore whether spiking neural networks can reduce computation by responding to changes while retaining the cues needed to recognize an event.

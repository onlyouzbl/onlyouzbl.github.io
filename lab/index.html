About the Lab

The Multimodal Understanding and Retrieval Lab (LIN-AI Lab) was founded at Ningbo University on November 5, 2026. Led by Bolin Zhang, the lab focuses on multimodal retrieval. We study how a query in one form, such as a sentence, can help us find relevant information in another, such as a video.

Research Areas

Cross-Modal Retrieval

Type “a sailboat on the water” and the system finds a matching picture. We study how to connect words with visual details so a query in one medium can retrieve results in another.

Video Moment Retrieval from Large Video Collections

Where in a whole collection of videos does a cat jump over a box? We search for the right recording and pinpoint the few seconds that show the action.

Recipe Retrieval

A plate of pasta can resemble several recipes. We match a food photo to ingredients and cooking instructions, using clues such as tomato pieces, basil leaves, and the shape of the pasta.

Cross-Modal Hashing

We encode images and text as short strings of bits. If a picture and a sentence describe the same thing, their codes should differ in few positions, making large collections faster to search.

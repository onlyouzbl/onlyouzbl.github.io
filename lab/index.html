About the Lab

The Multimodal Understanding and Retrieval Lab (LIN-AI Lab) was founded at Ningbo University on November 5, 2026. Led by Bolin Zhang, the lab focuses on multimodal retrieval. We study how a query in one form, such as a sentence, can help us find relevant information in another, such as a video.

Video Moment Retrieval

Given a description such as “a person pushes a bicycle across the street,” can a system find the moment the crossing begins and ends? We study how to match words to actions in long videos, including when the training examples have descriptions but no timestamps.

Video Anomaly Detection

Some events are hard to describe in advance. We study how to identify moments in long recordings that warrant a closer look. Context matters: a person running on a track may mean something different from a person running through a crowded station.

Spiking Neural Networks for Video

Consecutive video frames often contain much of the same information. We explore how spiking neural networks can represent changes over time and whether they can reduce computation while retaining the cues needed to recognize an event.
